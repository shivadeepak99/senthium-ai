# 💖 Why I Built Senthium

It started with something small — a coffee. ☕  
I just wanted to grab one.  
But my PC had other plans.

In the middle of a long build, it decided to **lock itself**.  
Compilation failed. Downloads stopped.  
My workflow — ruined. 😭

I couldn’t leave my PC **unlocked** (security).  
I couldn’t make it **never sleep** (wasteful).  
And “jiggle mouse” tools? They’re dumb hacks, not real solutions.

So I built **Senthium** — my smart little guardian. 💡

A background daemon that actually *understands* when I’m working:
- 💻 High CPU usage? Stay awake.  
- 🌐 Heavy download? Stay awake.  
- 🎬 Rendering? Training? Backups? Stay awake.  
- 😴 Done working? Let the system rest.

Senthium was born out of pure **pain**, a developer’s pain —  
those tiny everyday frustrations that eat time, energy, and sanity.

Now it’s become something bigger —  
a project that keeps *you* productive  
without compromising *your* security or *your* power savings.

It’s not just “prevent sleep.”  
It’s a small act of **care**, built into your system.  
Because your time is precious.  
And your coffee deserves peace. ☕💻

---

> 💬 **“Stay awake when it matters. Rest when it’s safe.”**  
> — Senthium, your intelligent system guardian 🦾
